## How the reviews work

Every review is of somewhere I actually went and paid for myself. Ratings run from 0 to 5 in half steps:

- **5:** I'd plan a trip around it.
- **4:** Very good. I'd happily go back.
- **3:** Fine for the price, nothing more.
- **2:** I wouldn't go again.
- **1 or below:** Avoid.
