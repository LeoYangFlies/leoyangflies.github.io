I'm Leo Yang, a high schooler from Shanghai who goes to school on Long Island. That makes me double-hubbed: home is split between New York and Shanghai, and a surprising amount of my life happens somewhere over the Pacific in between.

When you fly the same long route again and again, you start to notice everything: which seats are actually worth choosing, which meal is worth waking up for, which crews make fifteen hours feel shorter, and which entertainment systems freeze halfway through a movie. This site is where I write all of that down.

Mostly you'll find flight reviews, from the check-in desk to the baggage carousel. Sometimes I'll also cover the airports, the lounges, and the places I end up once I land.

## How the reviews work

Every review is of a flight I actually took. Ratings run from 0 to 5 in half steps:

- **5:** I'd pick this flight again over any other option.
- **4:** Very good. I'd happily book it again.
- **3:** Fine for the price, nothing more.
- **2:** I'd try to avoid it next time.
- **1 or below:** Not worth it at any price.
