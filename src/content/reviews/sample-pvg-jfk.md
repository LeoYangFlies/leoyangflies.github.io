This is a sample **flight review**. The flight details at the top of the page, the postmark and the "In short" box all come from the settings at the top of this file. Use the sections below as a checklist and delete the ones you have nothing to say about.

## Booking and check-in

How you booked, what it cost, how long the queue was, and anything unusual about bags or documents.

## Boarding

On time? Organised or chaotic? Was there room for your bag?

## The seat

Legroom, recline, the width, the window, the pillow and blanket. Would you pick the same seat again?

## Crew and service

How often did they come by? Anyone who stood out?

## Food

What were the options, what did you choose, and was it worth eating? Photos help a lot here.

## Entertainment and Wi-Fi

The screen, the movie selection, the headphones, whether Wi-Fi worked and what it cost.

## Arrival

Early or late, immigration, bags. How did you feel when you landed?
